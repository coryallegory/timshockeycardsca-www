Privacy

We keep only what your account needs: your email, a hash of your password, your collection, your sign-ins, your trade profile and who you follow. A public profile shows your trade id, your area if you add it, your collection and your external links; never your email. No name, no advertising or profiling. What we keep, why, for how long and the sign-in cookie are all on the Privacy page.
